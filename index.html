My Last Love Letter ❤️

My Ai,

Sometimes I wonder how someone so far away became the closest person to my heart.

We may live in different places, sleep under different skies, and wait through endless distances… but somehow your love still reaches me every single day.

You became my comfort in silence, my peace during hard days, and the reason ordinary moments started feeling beautiful again.

I still remember our first call on 9 October like it was yesterday.

Hearing your voice felt strange in the best way possible… like my heart already knew you before my mind did.

And slowly, without realizing it, you became my home.

Ai… Priyee… my Moon… my Princess…

I love every version of you.
Your smile.
Your voice.
Your little moods.
Your care.
Even the moments when you overthink or get emotional.

Distance is hard sometimes.
Really hard.

There are days when I wish I could simply hold your hand, sit beside you quietly, or just look at you without a screen between us.

But even after all that…
even after the waiting, the missing, and the silence…
my heart still chooses you every single time.

You once told me that you would always stay with me and love me.
So today, I want to promise you something too.

No matter how much distance exists between us…
no matter how life changes…
no matter how many years pass…
I will always love you.

In every late-night thought.
In every song.
In every star I look at.
In every version of my future…
there is always you.

And if someday the universe gives me another life again…
I think I would still search for your soul in every crowd until I found you.

Forever yours,
Anand ❤️
